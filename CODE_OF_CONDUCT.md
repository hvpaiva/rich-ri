# Code of conduct

Help make participation welcoming and constructive. Be respectful, explain
disagreements, accept corrections and focus criticism on work rather than people.
Assume that contributors have different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, personal attacks and publishing
another person's private information are not acceptable. These expectations
apply in project spaces and when representing the project publicly.

Report concerns privately to [contact@hvpaiva.dev](mailto:contact@hvpaiva.dev).
Include enough context to investigate, without sharing information unnecessarily.
The maintainer will handle reports discreetly and consider the impact and pattern
of behavior. Responses may include a request to change behavior, removing content,
a temporary restriction or a ban. People affected may ask the maintainer to
reconsider a decision and provide additional context.
